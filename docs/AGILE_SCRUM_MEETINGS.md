# Agile Scrum 3-5-3 Framework and Meetings

This document summarizes the core elements of Scrum (the "3-5-3"), and provides a categorized list of required and optional Agile/Scrum meetings for reference.

## Scrum 3-5-3 Framework

- **3 Roles:**
  - Product Owner
  - Scrum Master
  - Scrum Team Member (Devs, QA, Designer, Architect, DevOps, ...)
- **5 Events (Meetings):**
  - Sprint (the time-boxed iteration itself)
  - Sprint Planning
  - Daily Scrum (Standup)
  - Sprint Review
  - Sprint Retrospective
- **3 Artifacts:**
  - Product Backlog
  - Sprint Backlog
  - Increment

---

## Required Scrum Meetings (Events)

These are the official Scrum ceremonies that must be held in every Sprint:

- **Sprint Planning:** Define Sprint Goal, select and estimate work, clarify Acceptance Criteria.
- **Daily Scrum (Standup):** Team syncs on progress, blockers, and plans for the day.
- **Sprint Review:** Demonstrate the Increment, gather feedback from stakeholders.
- **Sprint Retrospective:** Inspect and adapt the process, identify improvements.
- **Sprint:** The time-boxed period in which the above events occur and work is delivered.

---

## Optional/Recommended Agile Meetings

These meetings are common in Agile teams but are not required by Scrum. They can add value depending on team needs and context:

- **Backlog Refinement (Grooming):** Ongoing review and prioritization of Product Backlog Items.
- **Product Demo (if not part of Sprint Review):** Broader demonstration for stakeholders or leadership.
- **Technical Spike/POC Review:** Review outcomes of technical investigations or proof-of-concepts.
- **Release Planning:** Plan for major releases beyond the Sprint scope.
- **Roadmap/Strategy Session:** Align on long-term vision and priorities.
- **Stakeholder Alignment Meeting:** Sync with business or external stakeholders.
- **Team Health Check/Working Agreement Review:** Assess team morale and update working agreements.
