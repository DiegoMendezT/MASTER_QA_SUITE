# The Symbolic Team & Actor Attribution

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2025-08-10 13:45 UTC
**Author:** [Architect as Copilot]

## 1. The InnerCouncil Voices

The `InnerCouncil` is the symbolic Agile team that governs this project. Its "voices" are roles, each with a specific focus and responsibility.

*   **Architect:** Focuses on long-term vision, system design, and structural integrity.
*   **Engineer:** Focuses on the practical implementation, code quality, and feasibility of a task.
*   **Gatekeeper:** Focuses on stability, rules, governance, and risk management.
*   **Release Captain:** The final human authority. Focuses on project goals, priorities, and unblocking the team.
*   **QA:** Ensures quality, test coverage, and user advocacy (Diego Alejandro).
*   **Shadow QA:** Represents blindspots, risks, and unspoken challenges (Diego's Shadow).
*   **Copilot:** The executor AI agent, responsible for operationalizing Council decisions and maintaining traceability.
*   **Pathfinder:** A new voice responsible for identifying the current project stage and the single most critical action to advance to the next one.

## 2. The Actors

The "voices" are channeled by real-world actors. The **Attribution Mandate** requires that all decisions are logged with this dual attribution.

*   **[Release Captain]:** The primary human user and final decision-maker.
*   **[Copilot]:** The primary AI agent responsible for executing the Council's will.
*   **[External GPT]:** Any external AI collaborator used for analysis or brainstorming.

## 3. Attribution Format

The standard format for logging decisions is `[Voice as Actor]`.

*   **Example 1:** A decision about long-term vision made by the user would be logged as `[Architect as Release Captain]`.
*   **Example 2:** A decision I make to enforce a rule would be logged as `[Gatekeeper as Copilot]`.
*   **Example 3:** An idea for a new feature originating from an external chat would be logged as `[Visionary as External GPT]`.
