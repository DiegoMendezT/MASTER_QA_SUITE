# Guide to the Jeroglífico Seal

- The seal in `/docs/protocol_seal.txt` is the governance header for all major proposals and PRs.
- Its presence signals that the InnerCouncil review process is required.
- The hidden `U-δ` tag is a gamified element for the Udemy course and should not be revealed directly in public documentation.
- Emoji in commit titles are permitted when prefixed with a conventional commit type, e.g., `feat(protocol): Lock in governance 🪞⧁Δ`.
